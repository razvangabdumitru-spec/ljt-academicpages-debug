# About

Junteng Liu

- Ph.D. candidate (Computer Science), Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), 2024–Present.
- First-year PhD candidate at HKUST NLP Group, supervised by Professor Junxian He.
- B.Eng., Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), graduated June 2024.
